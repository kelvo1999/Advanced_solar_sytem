Scale note

Planet diameters use a common linear scale relative to the Sun. The real planets are tiny at this scale, so click targets are larger than the drawn worlds. Orbital distances are compressed to fit the display. This is an educational visualization, not a live ephemeris.

*JPL Solar System Dynamics count includes planetary satellites of the planets and Pluto (updated 4 Nov 2026). Individual planet counts shown in the fact sheet are from NASA's planetary fact sheet (last updated 18 Mar 2025).
